ABOUT PANINKRET JAPAN

Connecting Ingredients,
Japanese Manufacturing, and Global Markets

Drawing on our expertise in functional ingredients, Paninkret Japan connects overseas brands with trusted Japanese manufacturers. From product planning and development to manufacturing and export coordination, we provide flexible, hands-on support to bring distinctive products to market.

OUR BUSINESS

What We Do

01

OEM & Product Development

We help bring your ideas to life through product planning, OEM coordination, wholesale distribution, and export support for cosmetics, hair care products, and health supplements.

02

Functional Ingredients

Leveraging our extensive expertise in functional ingredients, we import and distribute botanical and animal-derived extracts, extract powders, and oils to manufacturers and businesses throughout Japan.

03

Specialty Imports & E-commerce

Drawing on our import expertise, we source and distribute selected foods, finished products, and distinctive natural materials from overseas, while also operating e-commerce businesses in Japan.
